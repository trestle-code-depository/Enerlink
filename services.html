A Two-Way Energy and Infrastructure Corridor

Asia is not only a demand centre — it is home to world-class refining, shipbuilding, manufacturing and infrastructure capabilities. We facilitate selected opportunities in both directions.

North America → Asia

Energy supply to Asian markets

  • LNG long-term offtake and near-term cargoes
  • Crude oil and oil sands–derived crude streams
  • LPG, propane and butane cargoes
  • Sulphur and industrial commodities
  • Project equity, offtake-linked investment and infrastructure partnerships

Asia → North America

Refined products, capabilities & capital

  • Refined petroleum products — diesel, gasoline, jet fuel and other light products
  • Shipbuilding, marine and heavy-fabrication capabilities
  • Equipment, modules and energy technology
  • Strategic capital and project participation in North American energy

Principal focus: connecting Canadian and North American energy supply with Asian demand.

Our Services

Energy Market Representation & Offtake Development

Physical Energy & Commodity Market Facilitation

Connecting energy projects with investors, infrastructure partners, EPC capabilities, technology providers and strategic participants.

Cross-Border Market Development

How we work

A Protected Engagement Pathway

EnerLink’s role and authority vary by engagement and are defined in writing. Appropriate confidentiality and commercial protections are put in place before sensitive counterparty information is disclosed.

1

Qualification

Confirm the entity, decision-maker and commercial need.

2

Role Definition

Confirm EnerLink’s role, territory, scope and authority for the engagement.

3

Protection

Put appropriate confidentiality and commercial protections in place.

4

Commercial Alignment

Review product, volume, timing, logistics, pricing structure and counterpart requirements.

5

Strategic Introduction

Coordinate the appropriate protected introduction and commercial context.

6

Transaction Development

Support document flow and discussions toward MOU, term sheet, SPA or other definitive agreements.
