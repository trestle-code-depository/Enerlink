A Two-Way Energy and Infrastructure Corridor

Asia is not only a demand centre — it is home to world-class refining, shipbuilding, manufacturing and infrastructure capabilities. We facilitate selected opportunities in both directions.

North America → Asia

Energy supply to Asian markets

  • LNG long-term offtake and near-term cargoes
  • Crude oil and oil sands–derived crude streams
  • LPG, propane and butane cargoes
  • Sulphur and industrial commodities
  • Project equity, offtake-linked investment and infrastructure partnerships

Asia → North America

Refined products, capabilities & capital

  • Refined petroleum products — diesel, gasoline, jet fuel and other light products
  • Shipbuilding, marine and heavy-fabrication capabilities
  • Equipment, modules and energy technology
  • Strategic capital and project participation in North American energy

Principal focus: connecting Canadian and North American energy supply with Asian demand.

How we work

A Protected Transaction Pathway

We don’t rush sensitive disclosure. Buyer authority and seller protection are aligned before any name is released — this is the protection value EnerLink provides to both sides.

1

Qualification

Legal entity, decision-maker, real demand, financial capacity, purchasing authority.

2

Protection

NDA / NCNDA, LOI, mandate, fee protection and non-circumvention scope.

3

Matching

Volume, pricing formula, delivery terms, timing, terminal / spec requirements.

4

Introduction

Protected first contact; executive- and working-level connection.

5

Facilitation

Continuity

Trust, follow-on opportunities and long-term relationship continuity.
