Who we are

Built in Canada. Connected Across the Pacific.

EnerLink Group Inc. is a Canada-based energy representation and market development company headquartered in Calgary, Alberta — at the heart of Canada’s energy industry. Our Canadian base gives us proximity to the producers, project developers, marketers, infrastructure operators and industry specialists that shape Western Canada’s energy sector.

From this base, our reach extends across the integrated North American energy market. Across Asia, we develop relationships with qualified buyers, refiners, importers, strategic investors, EPC and industrial partners and relevant public-sector stakeholders. When appointed under a written mandate, EnerLink acts as the appointing company’s market representative within the agreed territory, sector and scope.

Why Canadian energy, why now

A new Pacific gateway

Canada is entering a new era of global energy market access. The expansion of the Trans Mountain system and the start of large-scale LNG exports from British Columbia have materially strengthened Western Canada’s access to Pacific and Asian markets.

Milestone 01 · Crude oil

Trans Mountain Expansion — May 2024

Nearly tripled system capacity, carrying Alberta crude and refined products to tidewater at Burnaby, BC for shipment to Asia and the U.S. West Coast.
